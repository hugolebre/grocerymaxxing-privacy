Privacy Policy — GroceryMaxxing

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the GroceryMaxxing mobile app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. GroceryMaxxing compares supermarket prices for a basket of everyday groceries.

Summary
  • The app collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and nothing about you is stored on our servers.
  • Your location never leaves your phone. It is only used, when you tap “near me”, to sort supermarkets by distance on the device.
  • Your shopping list and settings are kept only on your phone.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

What the app downloads

To show prices, the app downloads the current supermarket prices from our server. These requests contain no information about you. Like any internet request, they reveal your device’s IP address to the server, which uses it only to answer the request and does not store it with anything else.

Location

If you tap “near me”, the app asks for permission to read your approximate location. It is used on your device to sort stores by distance and pick the nearest store’s prices. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can refuse or withdraw the permission at any time in your phone’s settings; the app keeps working without it.

Data stored on your device

Your shopping list, chosen stores, language and a copy of the last prices downloaded are saved in the app’s private storage, so the app works offline. They are removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because we do not collect any.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that need personal data, such as an account or price alerts, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top revised.
